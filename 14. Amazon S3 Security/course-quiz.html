14·1U14-Q1Udemy quiz · sec 14 · Q1⚠ outdated

Your client wants to make sure that file encryption is happening in S3, but he wants to fully manage the encryption keys and never store them in AWS. You recommend him to use ............................

আপনার client নিশ্চিত হতে চান যে S3-এ file encryption হচ্ছে, কিন্তু তিনি encryption key পুরোপুরি নিজে manage করতে চান এবং কখনও AWS-এ store করতে চান না। আপনি তাঁকে ............................ ব্যবহার করার recommend করবেন

⚠ Outdated: April 2026 theke notun S3 general purpose bucket e SSE-C by default blocked. SSE-C use korte hole PutBucketEncryption diye (BlockedEncryptionTypes) SSE-C allow korte hoy. Concept hisebe answer SSE-C ekhono thik. source

Correct: C. Why it is right

SSE-C e key customer nije manage kore, AWS er baire rakhe, ar proti request e HTTPS header e key pathay. S3 oi key diye server-side encrypt kore, kintu key store kore na (PDF p. 320).

Tai "encryption S3 e hobe" + "key puro nijer, AWS e kokhono store hobe na" → SSE-C.

Aajker update: April 2026 theke notun general purpose bucket e SSE-C default blocked; use korte hole PutBucketEncryption (BlockedEncryptionTypes) diye allow korte hoy. Concept/exam answer ekhono SSE-C.

Why the other options are wrong

  • A. SSE-S3 e key AWS (S3) nije own ar manage kore; customer er kono key control nai.
  • B. SSE-KMS e key AWS KMS e store thake; customer control pay kintu key AWS er bhitore-i thake.
  • D. Client-side e encryption S3 e hoy na, client nije kore upload er age. Question chay encryption S3 e hok.

Keyword: encryption S3 e + key fully manage + never store in AWS → SSE-C

Instructor feedback (English)

A: With SSE-S3, the encryption happens in AWS and you the encryption keys managed by AWS. Encryption keys stored in AWS.

B: With SSE-KMS, the encryption happens in AWS, and the encryption keys are managed by AWS but you have full control over the rotation policy of the encryption key. Encryption keys stored in AWS.

C: With SSE-C, the encryption happens in AWS and you have full control over the encryption keys.

D: With Client-Side Encryption, you have to do the encryption yourself and you have full control over the encryption keys.

14·2U14-Q2Udemy quiz · sec 14 · Q2

A company you're working for wants their data stored in S3 to be encrypted. They don't mind the encryption keys stored and managed by AWS, but they want to maintain control over the rotation policy of the encryption keys. You recommend them to use ....................

আপনি যে company-তে কাজ করছেন, তারা চায় S3-এ store করা তাদের data encrypt করা থাকুক। encryption key AWS store ও manage করলে তাদের আপত্তি নেই, কিন্তু তারা encryption key-গুলোর rotation policy-র ওপর নিয়ন্ত্রণ রাখতে চায়। আপনি তাদের .................... ব্যবহার করার recommend করবেন

Correct: B. Why it is right

SSE-KMS e key AWS KMS e store ar manage hoy, kintu tumi KMS key er upor user control pao (rotation policy shoho), ar CloudTrail e key usage audit hoy (PDF p. 317).

Question: key AWS e thakle somossa nai, kintu rotation nije control korte chay → SSE-KMS.

Why the other options are wrong

  • A. SSE-S3 e key puro S3 er owned; rotation ba key er upor tomar kono control nai.
  • C. SSE-C e key customer AWS er baire rakhe; question bolche key AWS store/manage korbe.
  • D. Client-side e key ar encryption duto-i client er hate; AWS key manage kore na, requirement er ulta.

Keyword: key AWS e store/manage OK + rotation policy control → SSE-KMS

Instructor feedback (English)

A: With SSE-S3, the encryption happens in AWS and you don't manage the encryption keys. Encryption keys stored in AWS.

B: With SSE-KMS, the encryption happens in AWS, and the encryption keys are managed by AWS but you have full control over the rotation policy of the encryption key. Encryption keys stored in AWS.

C: With SSE-C, the encryption happens in AWS and you have full control over the encryption keys. You could define a rotation policy but this is against the question requirements (encryption keys must be stored and managed by AWS).

D: With Client-Side Encryption, you have to do the encryption yourself and you have full control over the encryption keys. You could define a rotation policy but this is against the question requirements (encryption keys must be stored and managed by AWS).

14·3U14-Q3Udemy quiz · sec 14 · Q3

Your company does not trust AWS for the encryption process and wants it to happen on the application. You recommend them to use ....................

আপনার company encryption process-এর জন্য AWS-কে বিশ্বাস করে না এবং চায় এটি application-এ হোক। আপনি তাদের .................... ব্যবহার করার recommend করবেন

Correct: D. Why it is right

Client-Side Encryption e client (application) nije data encrypt kore tarpor S3 te upload kore, ar download er por nije decrypt kore (PDF p. 321).

AWS kokhono plaintext ba key dekhe na. "AWS ke encryption process e trust kori na, application e hok" → client-side.

Why the other options are wrong

  • A. SSE-S3 e encryption AWS er server-side e hoy; question chay encryption application e hok.
  • B. SSE-KMS-o server-side: S3 AWS er bhitore encrypt kore, tai AWS ke trust korte hoy.
  • C. SSE-C te key tumi dao, kintu encryption ta S3 (AWS) e-i hoy; process AWS e thake.

Keyword: AWS ke trust kore na + encryption application e → Client-Side Encryption

Instructor feedback (English)

A: With SSE-S3, the encryption happens in AWS and you the encryption keys managed by AWS. Encryption keys stored in AWS.

B: With SSE-KMS, the encryption happens in AWS, and the encryption keys are managed by AWS but you have full control over the rotation policy of the encryption key. Encryption keys stored in AWS.

C: With SSE-C, the encryption happens in AWS and you have full control over the encryption keys.

D: With Client-Side Encryption, you have to do the encryption yourself and you have full control over the encryption keys. You perform the encryption yourself and send the encrypted data to AWS. AWS does not know your encryption keys and cannot decrypt your data.

14·4U14-Q4Udemy quiz · sec 14 · Q4

You have a website that loads files from an S3 bucket. When you try the URL of the files directly in your Chrome browser it works, but when a website with a different domain tries to load these files it doesn't. What's the problem?

আপনার একটি website আছে, যা একটি S3 bucket থেকে file load করে। আপনার Chrome browser-এ সরাসরি file-গুলোর URL চেষ্টা করলে কাজ করে, কিন্তু ভিন্ন domain-এর একটি website এই file-গুলো load করার চেষ্টা করলে কাজ করে না। সমস্যাটি কী?

Correct: C. Why it is right

Browser e file er URL directly khulle kaj kore, mane permission thik ache. Kintu onno domain er website load korte parche na → eta CORS problem (PDF p. 325-327).

Browser cross-origin request tokhon-i allow kore jokhon S3 bucket Access-Control-Allow-Origin header pathay. Fix: bucket e oi origin (ba *) er jonno CORS rule add koro.

Why the other options are wrong

  • A. Bucket policy bhul hole direct URL-o kaj korto na; ekhane direct access thik ache.
  • B. IAM policy bhul hole duto case-ei fail korto; direct access kaj korche, tai IAM na.
  • D. Encryption e problem thakle direct download-o fail korto; ekhane shudhu cross-domain fail.

Keyword: direct URL kaj kore + different domain theke fail → CORS

Instructor feedback (English)

A: If the S3 bucket policy is wrong, the files won't load in either case.

B: If the IAM policy was wrong, the files won't load in either case.

C: Cross-Origin Resource Sharing (CORS) defines a way for client web applications that are loaded in one domain to interact with resources in a different domain. To learn more about CORS, go here: https://docs.aws.amazon.com/AmazonS3/latest/dev/cors.html

D: If the encryption was wrong, the files won't load in either case.

14·5U14-Q5Udemy quiz · sec 14 · Q5⚠ outdated

An e-commerce company has its customers and orders data stored in an S3 bucket. The company’s CEO wants to generate a report to show the list of customers and the revenue for each customer. Customer data stored in files on the S3 bucket has sensitive information that we don’t want to expose in the report. How do you recommend the report can be created without exposing sensitive information?

একটি e-commerce company-র customer ও order data একটি S3 bucket-এ store করা আছে। company-র CEO customer-দের list এবং প্রতিটি customer-এর revenue দেখানো একটি report generate করতে চান। S3 bucket-এর file-এ store করা customer data-তে sensitive information আছে, যা আমরা report-এ expose করতে চাই না। sensitive information expose না করে কীভাবে report তৈরি করা যায় বলে আপনি recommend করবেন?

⚠ Outdated: Nov 7, 2025 theke S3 Object Lambda new customer der jonno available na (shudhu existing customer). AWS ekhon bole Lambda ke function URL / API Gateway / CloudFront diye call koro, ba client e data process koro. Course/exam er hisebe answer Object Lambda. source

Correct: A. Why it is right

S3 Object Lambda: caller application object retrieve korar age Lambda function object ke change kore (PDF p. 336). Use case: PII/sensitive data redact kora analytics er jonno.

Ekta bucket-i lage; S3 Access Point + Object Lambda Access Point er upor kaj kore, data er second copy rakhte hoy na.

Aajker update: Nov 7, 2025 theke Object Lambda notun customer der jonno available na (shudhu existing); AWS ekhon Lambda function URL / API Gateway / CloudFront ba client-side processing bole. Exam answer ekhono Object Lambda.

Why the other options are wrong

  • B. Kaj korbe, kintu second bucket e data copy, extra storage ar sync/maintain korar jhamela; Object Lambda ei kaj on-the-fly kore ek bucket e.
  • C. Object Lock WORM protection (delete/overwrite atkay); file er bhitorer sensitive field lukate pare na.

Keyword: report e sensitive info expose na kore, retrieve er age data change → S3 Object Lambda

14·6U14-Q6Udemy quiz · sec 14 · Q6

You suspect that some of your employees try to access files in an S3 bucket that they don't have access to. How can you verify this is indeed the case without them noticing?

আপনি সন্দেহ করছেন যে আপনার কিছু employee একটি S3 bucket-এর এমন file access করার চেষ্টা করছে, যেগুলোতে তাদের access নেই। তারা টের না পেয়ে কীভাবে আপনি যাচাই করতে পারেন যে আসলেই এটা ঘটছে?

Correct: A. Why it is right

S3 Access Logs S3 e asha shob request log kore, je account thekei hok, authorized ba denied, onno ekta S3 bucket e (PDF p. 329).

Tarpor Athena diye log file er upor serverless SQL query chalano jay. Kono access change hoy na, tai employee ra tero pay na.

Why the other options are wrong

  • B. IAM policy restrict korle employee ra access hariye bujhe jabe; question bole "without them noticing".
  • C. Bucket policy access block korbe, tai employee ra notice korbe; ar policy nije kono log dey na.

Keyword: unauthorized access verify + without them noticing → S3 Access Logs + Athena

Instructor feedback (English)

A: S3 Access Logs log all the requests made to S3 buckets and Amazon Athena can then be used to run serverless analytics on top of the log files.

B: This will be noticed by employees as they won't be able to access the files.

C: A bucket policy would prevent access to the user but they would notice it.

14·7U14-Q7Udemy quiz · sec 14 · Q7

You are looking to provide temporary URLs to a growing list of federated users to allow them to perform a file upload on your S3 bucket to a specific location. What should you use?

আপনি ক্রমবর্ধমান federated user-দের একটি list-কে temporary URL দিতে চান, যাতে তারা আপনার S3 bucket-এর একটি নির্দিষ্ট location-এ file upload করতে পারে। আপনার কী ব্যবহার করা উচিত?

Correct: B. Why it is right

Pre-Signed URL temporary URL: je user generate kore tar GET/PUT permission inherit kore, expiration thake (PDF p. 331).

Slide er example-i eta: "ever-changing list of users" ar "temporarily upload a file to a precise location". Tai growing federated user der specific location e upload er jonno pre-signed URL.

Why the other options are wrong

  • A. CORS shudhu browser ke onno origin er request allow kore; kono temporary permission ba URL dey na.
  • C. Bucket policy static; barte thaka dynamic user list ke temporary access deya jay na.

Keyword: temporary URL + growing list of users + upload to specific location → Pre-Signed URL

Instructor feedback (English)

A: Cross-Origin Resource Sharing (CORS) defines a way for client web applications that are loaded in one domain to interact with resources in a different domain. To learn more about CORS, go here: https://docs.aws.amazon.com/AmazonS3/latest/dev/cors.html

B: S3 Pre-Signed URLs are temporary URLs that you generate to grant time-limited access to some actions in your S3 bucket.

C: S3 Bucket Policies cannot help in giving temporary access to a dynamic list of federated users.

14·8U14-Q8Udemy quiz · sec 14 · Q8⚠ outdated

For compliance reasons, your company has a policy mandate that database backups must be retained for 4 years. It shouldn't be possible to erase them. What do you recommend?

compliance-এর কারণে আপনার company-র একটি policy নির্দেশ আছে যে database backup 4 বছর রাখতে হবে। এগুলো মুছে ফেলা সম্ভব হওয়া উচিত নয়। আপনি কী recommend করবেন?

⚠ Outdated: Purono vault-based Amazon Glacier service Dec 15, 2025 theke new customer ney na. Ekhon WORM backup er jonno S3 Glacier storage class + S3 Object Lock (Compliance mode) use kora hoy. Ei option gulor moddhe answer ekhono A. source

Correct: A. Why it is right

Glacier Vault Lock: WORM (Write Once Read Many) model. Vault Lock Policy banao, tarpor lock koro; ar edit/delete kora jay na, object delete hoy na (PDF p. 332). Compliance retention (4 bochor) er jonno perfect.

Aajker update: vault-based Amazon Glacier Dec 15, 2025 theke notun customer ney na; ekhon S3 Glacier storage class + S3 Object Lock (Compliance mode) use hoy. Ei option gulor moddhe answer ekhono A.

Why the other options are wrong

  • B. Linux permission root/admin chaile bodlate pare; WORM ba compliance guarantee nai, ar backup archive er jonno EFS dami.
  • C. Bucket policy pore keu edit ba remove korte pare; locked na, tai "erase kora possible na" guarantee dey na.

Keyword: retain 4 years + erase kora jabe na (compliance) → Glacier Vault Lock (WORM)

14·9U14-Q9Udemy quiz · sec 14 · Q9

You would like all your files in an S3 bucket to be encrypted by default. What is the optimal way of achieving this?

আপনি চান একটি S3 bucket-এর আপনার সব file by default encrypted থাকুক। এটি অর্জনের optimal উপায় কী?

Correct: B. Why it is right

Jan 2023 theke S3 sob notun bucket ar notun object automatically SSE-S3 diye encrypt kore (PDF p. 316, 324). Tai kichu korte hobe na; eta-i optimal.

Bucket policy diye shudhu specific encryption force korte hoy (jemon SSE-KMS); default encryption er jonno lage na.

Why the other options are wrong

  • A. HTTPS force (aws:SecureTransport) shudhu in-flight encryption dey; at-rest encryption na.
  • C. Versioning purono version rakhe; encryption er sathe kono somporko nai.

Keyword: encrypted by default + optimal → kichu na, S3 auto SSE-S3

Instructor feedback (English)

A: This will force in-flight encryption but not at-rest encryption.

14·10U14-Q10Udemy quiz · sec 14 · Q10

You have enabled versioning and want to be extra careful when it comes to deleting files on an S3 bucket. What should you enable to prevent accidental permanent deletions?

আপনি versioning enable করেছেন এবং একটি S3 bucket-এ file delete করার ব্যাপারে বাড়তি সতর্ক থাকতে চান। accidental permanent deletion ঠেকাতে আপনার কী enable করা উচিত?

Correct: B. Why it is right

MFA Delete chalu thakle object version permanently delete korte ba versioning suspend korte MFA code lage (PDF p. 328). Accidental permanent deletion er jonno extra protection.

Shorto: versioning enabled thakte hobe (ache), ar shudhu bucket owner (root) eta enable korte pare.

Why the other options are wrong

  • A. Bucket policy diye delete puro block kora jay, kintu tokhon dorkari delete-o hobe na; "extra careful" MFA er moto confirmation na.
  • C. Encryption data ke unreadable rakhe; delete atkay na.
  • D. Versioning disable korle ulta protection kome jay; purono version theke recover korar upay thake na.

Keyword: versioning on + prevent accidental permanent deletion → MFA Delete

Instructor feedback (English)

A: The bucket policy would prevent deletes altogether.

B: MFA Delete forces users to use MFA codes before deleting S3 objects. It's an extra level of security to prevent accidental deletions.

14·11U14-Q11Udemy quiz · sec 14 · Q11

A company has its data and files stored on some S3 buckets. Some of these files need to be kept for a predefined period of time and protected from being overwritten and deletion according to company compliance policy. Which S3 feature helps you in doing this?

একটি company-র data ও file কিছু S3 bucket-এ store করা আছে। company compliance policy অনুযায়ী এই file-গুলোর কিছু একটি পূর্বনির্ধারিত সময় পর্যন্ত রাখতে হবে এবং overwrite ও deletion থেকে protected রাখতে হবে। কোন S3 feature এটি করতে আপনাকে সাহায্য করে?

Correct: C. Why it is right

S3 Object Lock WORM model; versioning lage. Compliance mode e retention period er moddhe kono user, root-o object version overwrite ba delete korte pare na, mode bodlano ba period komano jay na (PDF p. 333).

Company compliance policy + predefined period + overwrite/delete theke strict protection → Compliance mode.

Why the other options are wrong

  • A. Governance mode e special permission wala user retention bodlate ba delete korte pare; compliance er strict guarantee na.
  • B. Versioning purono version rakhe, kintu keu version delete korte pare; fixed period er lock nai.
  • D. Glacier Vault Lock Glacier vault er jonno; question S3 bucket e rakha file er kotha bolche, tai S3 Object Lock.

Keyword: predefined period + overwrite/delete theke protect + compliance policy → Object Lock Compliance mode

14·12U14-Q12Udemy quiz · sec 14 · Q12

Which of the following S3 Object Lock configuration allows you to prevent an object or its versions from being overwritten or deleted indefinitely and gives you the ability to remove it manually?

নিচের কোন S3 Object Lock configuration একটি object বা তার version-গুলোকে অনির্দিষ্টকাল overwrite বা delete হওয়া থেকে আটকাতে দেয় এবং আপনাকে manually এটি সরানোর সুবিধা দেয়?

Correct: C. Why it is right

Legal Hold object ke indefinitely protect kore, retention period theke independent. s3:PutObjectLegalHold IAM permission thakle freely lagano ar remove kora jay (PDF p. 333).

"Indefinitely" + "manually remove" → Legal Hold.

Why the other options are wrong

  • A. Governance mode e ekta fixed retention period thake (indefinite na); period shesh hole lock shesh.
  • B. Compliance mode fixed period er jonno, ar keu (root-o) shorate ba komate pare na; manual remove possible na.

Keyword: indefinitely + remove manually → Legal Hold
